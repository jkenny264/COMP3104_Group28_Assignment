George Brown College is a public college in Toronto, Ontario, that offers career-focused programs combining academic education with practical, hands-on learning.

The college offers programs in various fields, including business, healthcare, design, and technology, helping students develop practical skills for their future careers.

There are 3 campuses:
Casa Loma Campus — 160 Kendal Avenue
St. James Campus — 200 King Street East
Waterfront Campus — 51 Dockside Drive
