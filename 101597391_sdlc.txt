DevOps in the Software Development Lifecycle
Contributor: Jocelyn Brown
Student ID: 101597391

Overview:

The software development lifecycle includes planning, requirements
analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
Teams may revisit these activities as requirements change and feedback
reveals opportunities for improvement.

DevOps supports collaboration throughout this lifecycle by connecting
development and operational responsibilities. Teams consider how software
will be tested, released, monitored, and maintained while designing
and implementing it.

Version control provides a shared history of changes. Automation makes
repeatable tasks more consistent, while feedback helps teams identify
problems and decide what to improve next.
